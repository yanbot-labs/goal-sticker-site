Privacy Policy

Goal Sticker · Last updated 29 July 2026

Goal Sticker does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. There are no accounts, no servers, and no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

The goals you create — their titles, due dates, reminder settings, and any optional notes, phone numbers or locations you add — are saved locally on your iPhone. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. We have no ability to see it.

What we collect

Nothing. Goal Sticker contains no analytics software, no advertising software, no crash reporting, and no tracking of any kind. The app makes no network requests as part of its normal operation.

Permissions the app may ask for

  • Notifications — so the app can remind you about a goal as its due date approaches. Notifications are scheduled and delivered entirely by iOS on your device.
  • Calendar (write only) — only if you choose "Add to Calendar" on a goal. The app can create an event; it cannot read your calendar.
  • Location — only if you attach a location to a goal. It is used to show a map and to save the address with that goal, and is stored on your device only.

Every one of these is optional. Declining any of them leaves the rest of the app fully usable.

Purchases

Goal Sticker offers a one-time in-app purchase to unlock additional features. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, and we receive no personal information about you from Apple. Apple's handling of that transaction is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Goal Sticker is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your iPhone removes everything it stored. Because nothing is held anywhere else, there is no separate deletion request to make and nothing for us to erase on our side.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
